# Third-party names and independence

MobileFilmLab is an independently maintained project. Device compatibility references and inherited recipe labels do not mean affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement or an official simulation. The project's app name, icon and interface are original; vendor logos and copied vendor artwork are not used as its branding.

## Is a descriptive name automatically prohibited?

Not automatically. Australian guidance distinguishes using a name as a **badge of commercial origin** from legitimate references that do not suggest that origin. Whether a particular use infringes depends on the context, relevant marks and goods/services. [1](https://ipfirstresponse.ipaustralia.gov.au/options/infringement-101-trade-mark-infringement)

Solely descriptive use is unlikely to be use as a trade mark, but a descriptive purpose does **not** automatically prevent a use from also functioning as a trade mark. [2](https://manuals.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademark/19a2-use-as-a-trade-mark)

This is general information, not a legal review or a guarantee that every recipe label, jurisdiction, store listing or presentation is permitted. A disclaimer alone does not establish permission. Obtain qualified advice if a rights holder raises a concern or before relying on a contested name commercially.

## Project practice

- The README uses general features, while prominently naming the primary supported-device target for compatibility information.
- Required upstream project names, authors, categories and licence notices remain in dedicated [attribution](../UPSTREAM_ATTRIBUTION.md) and [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- Inherited labels inside the app are descriptive; outputs are approximations, not exact recreations of proprietary colour science.
- No vendor logo, copied camera artwork, certification badge or endorsement claim is used as MobileFilmLab branding.
- If a specific label creates confusion or receives a substantiated complaint, review that presentation while preserving required provenance and licensing.
