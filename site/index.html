01 / The philosophy

Start with the light.
Not a prediction.

A natural starting point should be a starting point—not an effect you have to undo.

01

Let the camera be a camera.

The default Natural profile starts with the JPEG exposed by your device’s Camera2 pipeline. At 1×, with no edits enabled, it preserves the original JPEG pixel data and adds capture metadata.

No mandatory film grade. No mandatory enlargement.
02

No AI enhancement added.

MobileFilmLab adds no generative image model, neural photo enhancement or cloud processing. Optional colour, sharpening, grain and fusion use conventional, non-ML algorithms.

What this does—and doesn’t—mean
03

Your photos stay yours.

Preview, capture, looks and recipe processing happen on-device. No advertising, analytics or photo uploads. The app contacts GitHub only when you choose to check for an update.

Read the privacy notice

Hardware-first doesn’t mean an unprocessed sensor dump. The device’s ISP still processes the camera image. Manufacturer firmware and optional OEM modes may use proprietary processing we can’t inspect or rule out. This release saves JPEG, not RAW/DNG; “no AI added” describes MobileFilmLab’s own processing.

05 / A few honest answers

Before your
first frame.

No hidden magic.
No promises the hardware can’t keep.

Does the app use AI to process my photos?

MobileFilmLab adds no generative image model, ML enhancement or cloud photo processing. Its optional editing and fusion are conventional algorithms.

Your device’s camera/ISP still processes images, and optional manufacturer extensions may use proprietary techniques. We can’t promise that every firmware path is AI-free.

Does “hardware-first” mean RAW capture?

No. This release captures JPEG through Android Camera2; RAW/DNG export is not implemented. “Hardware-first” means starting from the exposed camera pipeline and avoiding app-added AI enhancement—not bypassing the ISP or accessing a bare sensor stream.

How do I keep the most natural starting point?

Use the default Natural baseline, standard Camera2 processing, native output and 1× framing, with grain off and lens gains unchanged. In that unedited path, the exposed JPEG pixel data is preserved and capture/recipe metadata is added.

Choosing a look, crop, grain or calibration edit changes the result. Those creative choices stay in your hands.

Will every rear camera work on my phone?

Only cameras and stream combinations exposed by the firmware can be used. Unavailable slots are disabled. The UW/W/3x/10x labels are navigation slots, not a guarantee of optical magnification.

Oppo Find X9 Ultra is the primary target. Please send a device report to help us expand compatibility.

Why is the screenshot scene AI-generated?

The screenshots render the app’s actual Android/Compose UI without a connected handset. A generated landscape provides a clearly labelled demo input; it was not captured or processed by the app. It demonstrates the interface, not photographic quality.

The project was also developed substantially with AI assistance. That is separate from the app’s photo-processing design. Read the development disclosure.

Is this a stable, universally tested camera app?

No. The current version is v0.2.0-beta.1, published as the latest regular GitHub Release by owner request. That classification does not turn it into a newly built stable version or certify every device and feature.

Keep another camera available for important photos and read the known limitations.
