# Historical design exploration

This directory preserves the original branding exploration, review boards and interface proposals. **These are design mockups, not screenshots of the implemented Android app.** Some include generated imagery or proposed controls that do not match the shipping interface.

The approved direction was original 35mm film-roll/canister branding and a spacious dark AUTO/PRO interface. The artwork is independently created; it does not copy upstream icons or camera-vendor logos.

For the current app, use the [README screenshot gallery](../README.md#screenshots) and its [provenance](../docs/SCREENSHOTS.md). Do not reuse these historical boards as evidence of real device capture or implemented features.

Files remain at their original paths to preserve historical review links. They are not needed for an Android build and are not packaged into the app.
